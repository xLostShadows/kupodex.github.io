2What Kupodex does

Kupodex brings Final Fantasy XIV character information into Discord. Its features include:

  • Character cards that show a character’s profile, jobs and progress from the Lodestone.
  • Character verification, which links your Discord account to a character you own and can set your nickname and roles.
  • Automatic role updates that recheck verified characters about once a day.
  • Raid sign-ups and looking-for-group posts, with reminders and pings for people who join.
  • Utility and fun commands, such as reset timers and a random job picker.

Features may be added, changed or removed over time.

6Character verification and Lodestone data

Kupodex reads only public information from the Lodestone and never asks for your Square Enix account or password.

To verify, you add a short code to your character’s Lodestone profile. Kupodex checks for the code, links the character to your Discord account in that server, and may set your nickname and roles. Codes expire after 30 minutes, and you can remove the code from your profile once you’re verified.

After you verify, Kupodex rechecks your character’s public Lodestone page about once a day. It updates your roles and nickname to match changes such as a new Free Company, a world transfer, a name change or finished expansion stories. Some features need your achievements to be public on the Lodestone.

Information shown by Kupodex comes from the Lodestone and other public sources. It may be delayed, incomplete or occasionally wrong.
