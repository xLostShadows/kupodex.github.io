Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

1Overview

Kupodex keeps only the data its features need, never sells it, and never reads your messages. This policy explains what Kupodex stores, why, how long it keeps it, and how to have it removed.

Kupodex is a Discord bot for Final Fantasy XIV communities, run by Nathan Dean (“we”, “us”). This policy works together with the Kupodex Terms of Service.

2What Kupodex stores

Kupodex stores the following, and nothing else about you:

DataWhyKept for
Verification link: your Discord user ID, the server ID, your Lodestone character ID and name, the IDs of roles Kupodex gave you, and when you verified and were last checked To keep your nickname and roles matched to your character Until you leave that server or ask us to remove it
Pending verification: your Discord user ID, the character you’re verifying and your code To check the code on your Lodestone profile Up to 30 minutes, in memory only
Raid posts: the organizer’s and sign-ups’ Discord user IDs and chosen roles, plus the post’s title, time, channel and message IDs To show sign-ups, send the reminder and close the post Until one day after the raid starts
LFG posts: the leader’s and members’ Discord user IDs and roles, plus the activity, note, channel and message IDs To show open spots and ping the group when it’s full Until the post fills, closes or expires (at most 4 hours)
Server settings: the server ID and the roles, Free Company ID and time zone its administrators chose To run Kupodex the way each server wants Until changed, or until we’re asked to remove them
Logs: short records of errors and activity, which can include Discord usernames, user IDs and character IDs To find and fix problems Rotated automatically, keeping about 30 MB of recent logs

Kupodex also keeps a few things in memory that aren’t about you, like job icons and game-wide mount and minion totals, so it doesn’t have to download them again.

3What Kupodex doesn’t collect

  • The content of your messages. Kupodex only sees the commands you run and the buttons you click.
  • Your Square Enix account, email address or password. Verification only uses a code you add to your public Lodestone profile.
  • Your IP address, device or location. Discord doesn’t share these with bots.
  • Payment information. Kupodex is free.

4How the data is used

Kupodex uses the data above only to provide its features: showing character cards, verifying characters, keeping nicknames and roles up to date, running raid and LFG posts, and fixing problems. It isn’t used for advertising, profiling or anything unrelated to Kupodex.

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing the features you and your server choose to use. You can object at any time by asking us to remove your data (see section 8).

5The Lodestone and public game data

Kupodex reads public pages on the Lodestone, Square Enix’s official Final Fantasy XIV site. It does this when someone views a card, when you verify, and about once a day for each verified member to keep their roles current.

Your character’s profile, jobs, achievements, mounts and minions are shown on the Lodestone to anyone. Kupodex shows this information in Discord but only stores the character ID and name listed above. Card images are created when requested and aren’t kept.

You control what the Lodestone shows, including whether your achievements are public, in your Lodestone account settings. Kupodex can only see what the Lodestone shows publicly.

6Sharing and third parties

We don’t sell, rent or trade your data. Kupodex works with these services:

  • Discord delivers commands, messages and roles. Discord’s Privacy Policy covers how Discord handles your data.
  • The Lodestone receives requests for public pages from our server. These requests don’t include your Discord information.
  • FFXIV Collect is asked only for game-wide mount and minion totals. No information about you is sent to it.
  • [hosting provider] hosts the server where Kupodex runs and its data is stored, in [country].

We’ll only share data beyond this if the law requires it.

7Storage and security

Kupodex’s data is stored on a private server that only we can access. The bot’s login token and other secrets are kept separate from its code. We keep the data to the minimum the features need, and remove it on the schedule above.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your data, we’ll let affected servers know through the support server or another reasonable channel.

8Your choices and rights

  • See or remove your data. Contact us (section 11) to get a copy of what Kupodex stores about you, or to have it deleted. We’ll respond within 30 days.
  • Unlink automatically. Leaving a server removes your verification link for that server at its next daily check.
  • Correct your data. Verifying again updates your link with your current character.
  • Limit what’s visible. Make your Lodestone achievements private to stop Kupodex from reading them. Some features, like expansion roles, won’t work without them.

Server administrators can ask us to remove their server’s settings and stored data, or simply remove Kupodex from the server.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under laws such as the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9Children

Kupodex is meant for people who meet Discord’s minimum age in their country. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under that age has used Kupodex, contact us and we’ll delete their data.

10Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when Kupodex starts handling data differently. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we’ll give notice in the Kupodex support server or another reasonable place before they take effect.
